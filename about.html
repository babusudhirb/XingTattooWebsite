About Us · Since 2021

About Xing Premium Tattoo Studio, Brookefield, Bangalore

We make forever look easy.

A tattoo is one of the few things you'll keep for life. We built Xing around that fact.

Xing opened in 2021 on the First Floor of SV Plaza, above DMART Ready, AECS Layout, Brookefield. The idea was simple: a custom tattoo studio for East Bengaluru where every design is drawn from scratch, every session is unhurried, and hygiene is treated like a clinic, not an afterthought.

Since then, 1200+ clients from Brookefield, Whitefield, Marathahalli, ITPL and beyond have trusted us with first tattoos, names and dates, memorial portraits, deity pieces, cover-ups and full sleeves — 1800+ tattoos and a 4.9★ Google rating later, the idea hasn't changed.

Every tattoo at Xing is designed and inked by one artist — Peace (Chingri Awungshi), with 15+ years of experience across realism, portraits, black & grey, fine-line, geometric and Indian traditional work. That means the person you consult with is the person who draws your design and the person who tattoos it.

We'd rather do fewer tattoos properly than rush more of them. It's why consultations are free and unhurried, why we'll give you honest advice on size and placement, and why a free touch-up is part of the deal if anything heals unevenly.

What We Stand For

Our Values

  • Design Before Ink

    Nothing goes on skin until the design is right. Every piece is sketched from scratch, sized to your body and reviewed with you first.

  • Clinic-Level Hygiene

    Single-use needles opened in front of you, sealed skin-tested inks and a sterilised, barrier-wrapped workstation for every client.

  • One Artist, Full Attention

    Every consultation, stencil and session is handled by Peace personally — no handoffs, no rotating chairs.

  • Honest Advice

    Straight answers on size, placement and how a design will age, even when that means reworking the idea.

  • Respect For Meaning

    Memorials, deities, names and scripts are treated with care — spelling checked, placement discussed, symbolism respected.

  • Aftercare That Continues

    Clear aftercare guidance to take home, and a free touch-up if any part of your tattoo heals unevenly.

Safety First

Hygiene & Safety Standards

A tattoo is a procedure that breaks the skin, so we run the studio like one. These standards apply to every client, every session — no exceptions.

  • 100% single-use, disposable needles — opened in front of you
  • Sealed, skin-tested inks imported from the US
  • Medical-grade sterilisation for every reusable tool
  • Barrier film on the machine, cables, bottles and workstation
  • Workstation fully cleaned and re-wrapped between every client
  • Health-regulation hygiene guidelines followed at every session
