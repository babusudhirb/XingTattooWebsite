## How do I choose between tattoo artists in East Bangalore?

Choose on healed work, hygiene and how well the artist listens — then check the practical side: hours, location and how easy it is to talk before you commit.

A quick shortlist method:

1. **Look at healed photos**, not just fresh ones.
2. **Check reviews** for comments about comfort, explanation and healing.
3. **Ask how consultations work** — free? Online? Evenings?
4. **Watch the hygiene** on your first visit.
5. **Notice the pressure level** — a good artist never rushes you.

## Can I fit a tattoo around a busy IT schedule?

Yes. Most small and medium tattoos need one session, and the key is to time it so the first few days of healing fall on your days off.

Here's how session length usually works:

| Tattoo type | Typical session time | Good slot for IT schedules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small minimalist or fine-line | About 30 minutes to 1.5 hours | Weekday evening |
| Geometric or traditional | Usually 1.5–4 hours | Saturday |
| Realism or portrait | 3–7 hours per sitting | Saturday, rest on Sunday |
| Sleeve or large piece | Several sessions | Weekends, a few weeks apart |

**Practical tips for working people:**

- Book after your release, not right before it.
- Avoid a session the night before a long flight or an onsite trip.
- Plan larger sessions for a Saturday so you can rest on Sunday.
- Block your calendar for the consultation like any other meeting.

## Which tattoo placements work with office dress codes?

The upper arm, shoulder, back, chest, ribs and thigh are easiest to cover. Forearms are covered by full sleeves; hands, fingers and neck are hardest to hide.

Most Bengaluru tech companies are relaxed about tattoos, but client-facing roles and some workplaces still prefer them covered. Think about:

- **Your current role** — are you client-facing or in on-site meetings?
- **Your next role** — would you want a different placement if you moved companies?
- **Travel** — some countries and clients are more conservative.
- **Your comfort** — do you want the option to show it, or hide it?

## Should I start minimalist or go straight for a sleeve?

Start with what you'll love in ten years. Many professionals begin with a small fine-line piece to see how tattooing and healing feel, then plan larger work later.

**Starting small suits you if:**

- You're unsure how you'll feel about visible ink at work.
- You want a short session that fits into an evening.
- You'd like to see how your skin heals first.

**Going bigger suits you if:**

- You've already got a clear theme or story.
- You're comfortable with multiple sessions over a few months.
- You want one connected design instead of scattered pieces.

## How do I heal a tattoo while working in an office?

Clean it gently morning and night, keep it covered with loose clothing during the day, and avoid rubbing it on your desk or laptop edge. Most people can work normally the next day.

**At the office:**

- Wash your hands before touching the tattoo.
- Wear loose sleeves — avoid tight cuffs, smartwatches or straps over the area.
- Keep it away from the desk edge and laptop palm rest if it's on your forearm.
- Carry a small aftercare product only if your artist recommends it.

**On the commute:**

- Cover arm tattoos from sun and dust on two-wheelers.
- Avoid packed buses where people may brush against a fresh tattoo.

**At home:**

- Wash gently with mild, fragrance-free soap and pat dry.
- Apply a thin layer of aftercare product.
- Sleep on clean sheets.

> **Peace's tip:** If you type all day and the tattoo is on your forearm, put a soft cloth or folded tissue under your wrist on the desk for the first week. It stops the fresh skin rubbing on hard edges.

## What professional standards should I expect from an artist?

Expect the same standards you'd expect at work: clear communication, honest advice, transparent pricing and a safe process. You should never feel pushed.

A professional artist will:

- Give you a **clear quote** before you commit.
- Tell you honestly if a design is **too small** or the placement won't age well.
- **Double-check spelling** on names, dates and script.
- **Open needles in front of you** and use sealed inks.
- Explain **aftercare in writing** or on WhatsApp.
- Offer a **touch-up** if part of the tattoo heals unevenly.

## What should I do on the day of the session?

Treat it like an important meeting: be rested, fed and on time, and clear the rest of your evening. A well-prepared body sits better and heals better.

**The day before:**

- Get a proper night's sleep.
- Skip alcohol for at least 24 hours.
- Moisturise the area lightly, but don't apply anything on the day.

**On the day:**

- Eat a full meal before you arrive.
- Drink water through the day.
- Wear loose clothes that give easy access to the placement.
- Bring a snack and water for longer sessions.
- Bring photo ID — you must be 18 or older.

**After the session:**

- Go home rather than back to the office if you can.
- Avoid heavy workouts that evening.
- Put your phone on silent for an early night; the post-tattoo tiredness is real.
