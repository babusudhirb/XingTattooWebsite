Our custom tattoo design process has seven clear steps: a free consultation, sketching from scratch, sizing to your body, stencil placement, a single-use setup, the session itself and aftercare follow-up. You approve every stage before anything goes on your skin.

At Xing Premium Tattoo Studio in Brookefield, Bengaluru, every tattoo is custom. This post walks you through exactly how a design goes from an idea in your head to ink on your skin.

## Step 1: the free consultation

Everything starts with a conversation. Your tattoo consultation is free, and there's no obligation to book.

**You can start in two ways:**

- **Visit the studio** in AECS Layout during opening hours.
- **Message us on WhatsApp** with your idea, references and placement.

**What we talk about:**

- Your idea and what it means to you.
- References — images you like, styles you love.
- Placement and rough size.
- Black and grey, colour or a mix.
- How you want it to look years from now.
- Any health questions, allergies or concerns.

Peace listens first, then gives honest advice — including if a design needs more space, a different placement or a simpler approach to age well.

## Step 2: sketching from scratch

Once we understand your idea, Peace sketches a design from scratch. Nothing is copied from the internet.

- Your references are used for inspiration, not tracing.
- Personal details — dates, initials, symbols — are built in.
- Any lettering is drawn carefully, and regional scripts are checked against your source.

This is where your tattoo becomes yours alone. Our guide to [custom vs flash tattoos](/blog/custom-vs-flash-tattoo) explains why this matters.

## Step 3: sizing to your body

A design on paper isn't a tattoo yet. It has to fit your body.

- **Size** is chosen so details stay clear as the tattoo ages.
- **Shape** is adjusted to follow your arm, back, ribs or wherever it goes.
- **Flow** is checked so the design moves naturally with you.

If a design needs to be a little bigger to stay sharp, we'll tell you why.

> **Peace's tip:** I often print the design in two or three sizes and hold each against your skin. Seeing the real size on your body makes the decision much easier than imagining it on a screen.

## Step 4: stencil placement

Before any tattooing, the design is transferred onto your skin as a stencil.

1. The area is cleaned and, if needed, shaved with a fresh disposable razor.
2. The stencil is applied.
3. You check it in the mirror — standing, sitting and moving.
4. If it's not quite right, we remove it and place it again.

Nothing starts until you're completely happy with the placement.

## Step 5: single-use setup

Hygiene is non-negotiable. Before your session:

- **Single-use, disposable needles** are opened from sealed packs in front of you.
- **Sealed, skin-tested inks** are poured into fresh single-use caps.
- **Barrier film** covers the machine, cables, bottles and workstation.
- **Fresh gloves** are worn and changed when needed.
- **Reusable tools** go through medical-grade sterilisation.
- The **workstation is cleaned and re-wrapped** between every client.

You're welcome to watch every step. Our [hygienic tattoo studio checklist](/blog/hygienic-tattoo-studio-checklist) explains why each one matters.

## Step 6: the session

Peace tattoos every piece personally — no handoffs between artists.

- Outlines first, then shading or colour.
- Breaks whenever you need them.
- Each step explained if you'd like to know what's happening.
- Larger pieces are planned across sessions, with healing time between.

Bring water and a snack for longer sessions, and wear comfortable clothes that give easy access to the placement. Our [tattoo appointment prep checklist](/blog/tattoo-appointment-prep) covers what to do before.

## Step 7: aftercare follow-up

The process doesn't end when you leave the chair.

- Your tattoo is cleaned and wrapped.
- You get clear aftercare guidance to take home.
- You can message us on WhatsApp with photos if anything worries you while healing.
- If any part heals unevenly, we offer a free touch-up.

Our [tattoo aftercare guide](/blog/tattoo-aftercare-guide) walks you through all 30 days.

## Large pieces and multi-session designs

For sleeves, back pieces and other large tattoos, the process is the same but spread out.

- The full design is planned at the start, even if it's tattooed in stages.
- Each session focuses on one area — usually outlines first, then shading and detail.
- Your skin heals between sessions, so each new layer goes into healthy skin.
- We review the design together before each session in case anything needs adjusting.

## Cover-up and rework designs

If you're covering or reworking an old tattoo, we start by looking at the old ink in good light. The new design is drawn around it, with darker areas planned where the old tattoo is heaviest. We'll be honest about what's possible, and sometimes suggest laser lightening first. Our [tattoo cover-up guide](/blog/tattoo-cover-up-guide) explains more.

## Why we don't rush the design

A tattoo lasts a lifetime, so a few extra days spent on the design is time well spent. Rushing the sketch can mean a design that doesn't fit your body, lettering that isn't quite right or details that blur as the tattoo ages. We'd rather take the time to get it right.
