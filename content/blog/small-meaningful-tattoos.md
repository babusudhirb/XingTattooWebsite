## Making symbols personal

A common symbol becomes uniquely yours with a small personal touch.

- **Add a date** — the day something changed.
- **Add initials** — of someone you love.
- **Use handwriting** — a parent's or partner's.
- **Change the style** — a geometric lotus, a single-line heart.
- **Combine two symbols** — an arrow through a lotus, a moon with an Om.
- **Choose a meaningful placement** — over the heart, on the hand you write with.

> **Peace's tip:** Tell me why you want the symbol, not just which one. A lotus for a new start might look different from a lotus for a grandmother. The reason shapes the design.

## Tattoo symbols meaning: choosing wisely

- **Research the symbol** so you understand its common meanings.
- **Avoid symbols with unintended meanings** in other cultures.
- **Choose something you'll still relate to** years from now.
- **Don't choose purely for a trend.**

## Size and longevity

Small tattoos still need to be big enough to last.

- **Simple shapes** can be very small.
- **Detailed symbols** — like a lotus or compass — need more space.
- **Text** needs height so letters stay open.
- **Leave space between lines** to stop them merging over time.

## Colour in small tattoos

Most small symbols are black, but a touch of colour can make them special — a red heart, a blue wave or a golden sun. Choose deep, saturated shades that show well on your skin tone, and keep colour minimal so the design stays crisp.

## Healing small tattoos

Small tattoos heal quickly, but they still need proper aftercare. Wash gently, moisturise lightly and keep them out of the sun for the first couple of weeks. Small designs on the wrist and fingers get washed often, so reapply a thin layer of aftercare product after washing your hands. Our [tattoo aftercare guide](/blog/tattoo-aftercare-guide) covers the full routine.

## Choosing your symbol

If you're torn between several symbols, write down what you want the tattoo to remind you of. Then match that feeling to a symbol from the list above. The simplest choices are often the ones you'll love longest. If two symbols both feel right, ask your artist whether they can be combined into one small, balanced design rather than two separate tattoos.
