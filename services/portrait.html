Healing Guide

Portrait Aftercare Timeline

Every style heals a little differently. Here's exactly what to expect with your portrait tattoo, stage by stage.

Day 1–3

Fresh & Fragile

  • Wash gently twice a day with fragrance-free soap
  • Pat dry and apply a thin layer of aftercare ointment
  • Avoid touching the face/detail zones of the tattoo unnecessarily

Day 4–14

The Peel

  • Avoid rubbing near fine-detail areas like eyes and hair strands — they're the most fragile part
  • No direct sun on the healing tattoo
  • Keep moisturising lightly, but don't over-saturate — it can blur fine lines

Week 2–4

The Itch

  • Do not pick, especially around fine linework — this is where likeness is lost
  • Continue gentle, fragrance-free moisturising
  • Avoid pools, saunas and prolonged sun exposure

Month 2–3

Settle & Shine

  • Book a touch-up if any softening appears around the eyes or lips
  • Start daily SPF over the piece — detail areas fade first without it

General guidance only — always follow the printed aftercare sheet given after your session, and contact us if anything looks infected rather than simply healing.

How long does a portrait tattoo take?

A portrait tattoo at Xing Premium Tattoo Studio in Brookefield, Bengaluru, usually takes 3–7 hours per sitting, depending on size, detail and placement.

A single small face can often be finished in one sitting; larger or multi-person portraits are split across sessions, with healing time in between.

Peace (Chingri Awungshi) gives you a time estimate at your free consultation. Breaks are built in, and you can pause whenever you need to. Eat a proper meal beforehand and keep the day free so nothing feels rushed.
