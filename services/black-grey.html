Healing Guide

Black & Grey Aftercare Timeline

Every style heals a little differently. Here's exactly what to expect with your black & grey tattoo, stage by stage.

Day 1–3

Fresh & Fragile

  • Wash gently twice a day with fragrance-free soap
  • Pat dry, then apply a thin layer of aftercare ointment
  • No soaking or long showers

Day 4–14

The Peel

  • Scabbing over shaded zones is normal — pat, never pick
  • Patchy grey is the single most avoidable touch-up request, and it comes from picking
  • Switch to a fragrance-free lotion once ointment stage ends

Week 2–4

The Itch

  • Skin can look "foggy" while healing — true depth returns after full regeneration
  • Avoid direct sun, pools and saunas
  • Keep the area moisturised without over-saturating

Month 2–3

Settle & Shine

  • SPF is critical — black & grey shows sun-fading as blotchy grey patches faster than solid black
  • Book a touch-up for any uneven shading

General guidance only — always follow the printed aftercare sheet given after your session, and contact us if anything looks infected rather than simply healing.

How long does a black & grey tattoo take?

A black & grey tattoo at Xing Premium Tattoo Studio in Brookefield, Bengaluru, takes anywhere from one short sitting to several sessions, depending on size, detail and placement.

Smaller shaded pieces often fit in a single sitting, while detailed black & grey realism runs 3–7 hours per sitting and larger work is split across sessions.

Peace (Chingri Awungshi) gives you a time estimate at your free consultation. Breaks are built in, and you can pause whenever you need to. Eat a proper meal beforehand and keep the day free so nothing feels rushed.
