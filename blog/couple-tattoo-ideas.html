The best couple tattoo ideas are small, meaningful and built to last: a shared symbol, an important date, the coordinates of a special place, or two designs that complete each other. They celebrate your relationship without feeling like a trend.

Couple tattoos are popular at our studio in Brookefield, Bengaluru — especially for anniversaries, engagements and milestone birthdays. Many first-timers get their first tattoo as a couple.

Here's how to choose a design you'll both love for years.

Matching vs complementary tattoos#

There are two main approaches to couple tattoos.

Matching tattoos are identical. Both partners get the same design, often in the same spot.

Complementary tattoos are two different designs that belong together — like a lock and key, a sun and moon, or two halves of one picture.

Matching Complementary
Design Identical Two parts of one idea
Feel Unity, sameness Balance, completing each other
Flexibility Simple to plan More room for personal style
Examples Same word, same symbol Sun & moon, lock & key, puzzle pieces

Both are beautiful. Choose the one that feels like your relationship.

Couple tattoo ideas: symbols#

Symbols carry meaning in a small space and are easy to personalise.

  • Infinity sign — love without end.
  • Heart outline — simple and classic.
  • Sun and moon — two people who balance each other.
  • Lock and key — belonging together.
  • King and queen crowns — playful and bold.
  • Anchor and compass — stability and direction.
  • Two halves of a heart — each partner carries one.
  • Puzzle pieces — fitting together perfectly.
  • Arrows — moving forward together.
  • Lotus — growth and rising together through challenges.

Dates, coordinates and words#

These matching tattoos are personal and flexible.

  • Your wedding or anniversary date in numbers or Roman numerals.
  • The date you met or got engaged.
  • Coordinates of where you met, got married or had your first home.
  • A shared word — "always", "forever", "together" — in each other's handwriting.
  • A phrase in your mother tongue that only you two understand.
  • A song lyric or line from a film you love.

Dates and coordinates are a great choice because they mark a moment you'll always share, whatever happens.

Minimal couple designs#

Most couple tattoos are small and fine-line. They're quick, usually less painful, and easy to place.

Popular minimal ideas:

  • Tiny matching hearts on the wrist.
  • Two small stars or a constellation.
  • A single continuous line that connects across two wrists when you hold hands.
  • Small initials inside a symbol.
  • Matching waves, mountains or flowers.

Our list of minimalist tattoo ideas has more small designs that work well for couples.

Peace's tip: If you want designs that connect when you hold hands or stand together, bring your partner to the consultation. I'll place the stencils on both of you at the same time so the lines meet perfectly.

Placement for couple tattoos#

Placement Why couples choose it
Inner wrist Visible to both of you, easy to match
Ring finger Symbol of commitment (fades faster)
Forearm Room for words, dates or small designs
Behind the ear Small and private
Ankle Discreet and delicate
Over the heart Deeply personal
Back of the shoulder Easy to hide

You don't have to choose the same spot. Many couples pick the same design in places that suit each person's work and comfort.

Anniversary gift tattoos#

A tattoo can be a meaningful anniversary gift — but plan it together.

  • Don't surprise your partner with a booking. Make sure they want a tattoo and choose the design together.
  • Pick a date that matters — your anniversary week or a milestone year.
  • Book the consultation together so both designs are sized and placed well.
  • Allow time for healing before beach trips or holidays.

Some couples also choose a tattoo to celebrate a new chapter — moving in together, a wedding, or the birth of a child.

Thinking long-term#

Couple tattoos celebrate love, and it's worth thinking about the future too.

  • Choose symbols or dates rather than names if you want flexibility.
  • Make it meaningful to you as an individual as well as a couple.
  • Keep it small if you're unsure.
  • Choose a design you'd still like on its own.

If you have an old couple tattoo you'd like to change, our tattoo cover-up guide explains your options.

Planning your couple tattoo session#

  • Book one consultation for both of you.
  • Agree on the design, size and placement together.
  • Book back-to-back sessions on the same day if you'd like.
  • Both of you should eat, sleep and hydrate well beforehand.
  • Bring photo ID — you must both be 18 or older at most studios.
  • Plan two weeks without swimming or long sun exposure.

Our first tattoo guide for Bengaluru covers everything else first-timers need to know.

Couple tattoo checklist#

  • We've both agreed on the idea.
  • We've chosen matching or complementary designs.
  • We've considered symbols or dates instead of names.
  • Each of us is happy with our placement.
  • We've planned around work, family and travel.
  • We've booked a joint consultation.

Couple tattoos inspired by Indian traditions#

Many couples in India draw on shared culture and faith for their designs:

  • Shiva and Parvati symbols — a trishul and a lotus, representing balance and partnership.
  • Ardhanarishvara-inspired halves — two designs that form one whole.
  • Each other's names in your mother tongue — Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Bengali or any language you share.
  • A small Om or lotus in matching placements.
  • Your wedding date in Devanagari numerals.

If you use any script or spiritual symbol, check spelling carefully and choose a respectful placement. Our guide to Indian traditional tattoos explains more about spiritual designs.

Healing together#

Healing at the same time can actually be fun — you can remind each other to moisturise and not scratch. Keep your aftercare products separate, wash your hands before touching your own tattoo, and avoid pressing your healing tattoos together for the first couple of weeks.
