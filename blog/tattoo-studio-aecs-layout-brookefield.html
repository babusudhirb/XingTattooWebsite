Why do locals choose Xing over ready-made flash?#

Most of our neighbours come in wanting something that means something to them — and a sheet of pre-drawn flash on a wall rarely does that. At Xing, every design is drawn from scratch by Peace.

That doesn't mean you have to arrive with a finished idea. Most people bring:

  • A few screenshots from Instagram or Pinterest.
  • A word, date, name or symbol that matters to them.
  • A rough idea of where it should go.

Peace takes those pieces and turns them into a design sized for your body. A lotus for the inner forearm is drawn differently from a lotus for the spine, because the skin, curve and movement are different.

Who will tattoo me at Xing?#

Peace will — every time. He is the studio's lead artist, with 15+ years of experience and 1800+ tattoos completed for 1200+ clients.

This matters more than people expect. At larger shops, the person who takes your booking, the person who draws your design and the person holding the machine can all be different. Small details get lost between them.

At Xing there are no handoffs. Peace hears your story at the consultation, draws the sketch, adjusts it with you, prepares the stencil and does the tattoo. You can read more about his background on the tattoo artist page.

Peace's tip: Bring the "why" with you, not just the picture. When I know a design is for your grandmother, your first job or your dog, I can make small choices — a line weight, a flower, a date — that make it feel like yours.

Is the AECS Layout studio hygienic and safe?#

Yes — and you can see it for yourself. Needles are single-use and opened in front of you, inks are sealed and skin-tested, and the workstation is cleaned and re-wrapped between every client.

Here is what our hygiene setup includes, as listed on our about page:

  • Single-use, disposable needles — opened in front of you.
  • Sealed, skin-tested inks imported from the US.
  • Medical-grade sterilisation for every reusable tool.
  • Barrier film on the machine, cables, bottles and workstation.
  • A full clean and re-wrap of the workstation between clients.

You don't have to take our word for any of this. Ask questions, watch the setup and look around. Our hygienic tattoo studio checklist explains what each step is for, and our new guide to safe tattoo needles and sterilisation shows exactly what to look for at the needle stage.

What do reviews say about this Brookefield tattoo studio?#

Xing holds a 4.9★ rating from 200+ Google reviews. Many come from first-timers who mention feeling comfortable and getting clear aftercare advice.

When you read reviews for any studio, look past the stars. The useful details are:

  • Do people mention the same artist consistently?
  • Do they talk about how the tattoo healed, not just how it looked on day one?
  • Do first-timers say they felt listened to rather than rushed?

A high rating is a good sign. Reviews that describe healed results and a calm experience are a better one. And if a review raises a question, ask about it at your consultation — a good studio is happy to explain.

What happens at a walk-in consultation?#

You sit down with Peace, talk through your idea, and leave with a clear plan and quote — with no obligation to book. It's free.

A typical consultation covers:

  1. Your idea — what you want and why it matters.
  2. Placement — where it will sit, how visible it will be and how it will age.
  3. Size — big enough for the detail to last.
  4. Skin check — scars, moles, old tattoos or sensitive areas.
  5. Time and price — how long the session will take and a clear quote.

If you work in one of the nearby tech parks, you can start the conversation on WhatsApp during the day and come in after work. Our post on what happens at a tattoo consultation walks through each step.

How we do it at Xing#

Every visit follows the same calm, clear process:

  1. Free consultation in the studio or on WhatsApp.
  2. Custom sketch drawn from scratch by Peace.
  3. Design review — you request changes before anything is booked.
  4. Session day — single-use needles opened in front of you, sealed inks, barrier-wrapped station.
  5. Aftercare — clear instructions, WhatsApp support while you heal, and one free touch-up within 45–60 days if any part heals unevenly.

There's no rush and no upselling, and you're welcome to think the design over before you book. If an idea won't age well at the size you want, Peace will tell you honestly and suggest an alternative.
